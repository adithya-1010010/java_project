# Glossary

- **Show** — a movie presented at a theatre at a specific time.
- **Booking** — a customer's confirmed purchase of seats for one show.
- **Booking ID** — unique identifier generated for each confirmed booking.
- **Seat** — a position in the theatre's row/column grid, tied to a show for availability.
- **Repository / DAO** — database access layer.
- **Service** — business rules and orchestration layer.

Grow as needed.
